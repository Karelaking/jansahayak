# JanSahayak SafeStreets

## Visual direction

The public landing page is a civic editorial surface: calm, warm, and evidence-minded rather than bureaucratic or alarmist. Its visual world is built from the feeling of a well-kept neighbourhood noticeboard translated into a modern public service: generous paper-like space, forest-green structure, sage map surfaces, and small safety accents in amber and coral.

Headlines use an editorial serif voice to feel human and memorable. Supporting text and controls use a highly readable sans-serif voice. The signature is the illustrated safety-map surface: real page content sits inside a restrained, clearly labelled map view instead of using fabricated statistics or stock imagery.

## Product and surface rules

- The first viewport must explain the service in one pass and make “Report a hazard” unmistakable.
- “Explore the map” is the secondary action and must remain equally findable.
- The Greater Noida pilot is visible without implying broader coverage.
- The platform complements official grievance channels and must never imply government authority.
- Illustrative map content is labelled as illustrative until connected to real data.
- Avoid fear-based imagery, generic charity tropes, dashboards, unsupported impact claims, and invented statistics.
- Keep the interface accessible without imagery and usable on small screens.
